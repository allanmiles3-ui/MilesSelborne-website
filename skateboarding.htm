Written by Deane Ketzner

During the year the skateboarders have been ripping it up at Selborne College. It has been a successful year for most of us. At the beginning of the year David Mc Gregor, Grade 8, got sponsored by a well known skateboarding brand called Premium. Like most of us, Sinclair Sumner got sponsored by his parents. There was a skateboarding competition held at our local skate park in April. Results were as follows: David Mc Gregor 1st and Deane Ketzner 2nd. Sinclair Sumner had to enter at pro level and he came 2nd.  

It has been a really good year for all of us. We skateboarders would like to thank Mr Miles (teacher in charge) and Dale Shaw (Society Chairman) for making this all happen. Our practicing during breaks helps a lot.
