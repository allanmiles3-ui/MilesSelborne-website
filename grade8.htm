4.1  Starting a business

In order to start a business one has to have money. The person starting a business can contribute the money or obtain the money from other sources.

The person contributing the money to the business will be known as the owner and will be regarded as an investment in the business. The money the owner contributes toward his business will be known as his capital contribution. This will reflect the amount of interest the owner has in his business known as his owner’s equity.

Before one can open a business certain decisions must be taken by the owner.

These decisions are:

  1. Kind of business activity – this will depend upon certain criteria namely:

Skills, training and experience of the prospective entrepreneur

  1. Type of ownership – is the owner going to go it alone or is he going to get others to come in the business venture with him. (Options available to him sole trader, partnership, close corporation, public company or private company).

 

  1. Suitable name for the business – the name must be acceptable to the owner and future customers.

 

  1. A suitable site must be found to conduct business from – business be located in easy reach to customers, and should have parking.

 

  1. Size of building required – this will depend on the type of business being conducted. The owner must also decide if the building will be rented or bought. 

 

  1. Equipment required – this will depend on the type of business being conducted. The owner must decide if the equipment will be leased or bought.

 

  1. Determine capital required – this will be determined by taking the above steps into account

 

The owner must also make the following arrangements:

-         Decide on an opening date

-         Obtain the required licenses

-         Obtain insurance cover

-         Arrange for the rendering of services

-         Have the required stationery and documents

-         Recruit the right personnel

-         Register with state departments

-         Open a current banking account

 

Homework                                                                                                                                                           Exercise 1

You are going to start a business. You must decide upon the following:

1.1  What type of business are you going to open? (service or trading)

1.2  Explain why you decided on this type of business activity?

1.3  Decide on a suitable name for your business?

1.4  How much capital do you think you will need to start your business, and show how you arrived at that amount?

1.5  Where will you get the capital from to start your business? Name at least three other sources besides contributing the money yourself.
