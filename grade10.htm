The most important concept you need to grasp before we start with this years syllabus is the Accounting Equation.

                                                           PROVING THE ACCOUNTING EQUATION                                                    

Net Worth

n      Your Net Worth is what you are worth after you have deducted what you owe from what you own.

n      Net Worth = What you own – What you owe

n      NET WORTH = ASSETS – LIABILITIES

Double Entry Principle 

n      From the above example it can be seen that every financial transaction has a double-sided effect.

n      If clothes are bought…

n      Clothes will change and Bank will change

n      Consequently, the Accounting Equation will always be in balance.

n      ASSETS = NET WORTH + LIABILITIES

Expanded Accounting Equation 

n      Net Worth = Assets – Liabilities

n      Assets = Net Worth + Liabilities

n      Assets = Owner’s Equity + Liabilities

n      Note: Owner’s Equity = Capital + Income – Expenses – Drawings

n      Assets = Capital + Income – Expenses – Drawings + Liabilities

n      Assets + Expenses + Drawings = Capital + Income + Liabilities
