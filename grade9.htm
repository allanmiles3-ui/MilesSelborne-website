Module 1                                                 THE TRADING UNDERTAKING                                                                 

Learning outcomes: to enable learners to

-         understand the concept – ‘Trading Undertaking’

-         understand the concept mark-up on cost price and to make calculations around this concept

-         communicate with others using both mathematical and language skill

-         enter transactions specific to Trading Undertakings in the Cash Journals, General Ledger and prepare a Trial Balance

-         enter transactions specific to combined undertakings

A trading undertaking can be defined as a business that buys goods and then resells them at a profit.

The goods bought for the purpose of resale are known in accounting as Trading Stock and are a Current Asset to the business.

When goods are sold, the selling of the goods is known as Sales. This is an income to the business. Trading stock represents the Cost Price of the goods. The difference between the cost price and the selling price is the Gross Profit made by the business.

When goods are sold by the business both the selling price and cost price of the goods sold must be calculated. The cost price of the goods sold in accounting terms is known as Cost of Sales. The profit made by the business usually represents a percentage of the cost price.

           COST PRICE =          100                   X                           Selling Price

                                        100 + Mark Up                                             1

                    PROFIT = SELLING PRICE - COST PRICE

           

            MARK UP % =           PROFIT               X                               100
